A live problem surfaces from inside relations: through a material's resistance, an unexpected question, a friction that doesn't neatly resolve. It keeps the practice alive rather than closing it down into a predetermined outcome.

This portfolio gathers practices of making, teaching, designing, and writing. They do not belong to a single discipline. They are ongoing.
