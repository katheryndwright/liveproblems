A live problem surfaces from inside relations: through a material's resistance, an unexpected question, a friction that doesn't neatly resolve. It requires a practice that stays inside the encounter long enough for something to be made from it.

This portfolio gathers practices of making, teaching, designing, and writing. It does not belong to a single discipline. These practices are situated in place, in popular culture, and move between analog and digital. It is ongoing.
